The core loop

A round takes seconds. A session takes minutes.

Choose a World, see the challenge, guess, get rewarded — then do it again. Every round is quick, colorful, and encouraging.

1

Choose a World

Animals, Dinosaurs, Space, Food, and more — each with its own colors and characters.

2

See the challenge

A picture reveals slowly, a silhouette sharpens, or a sound plays — seven ways to play in all.

3

Guess — no pressure

Early guesses score more. Wrong guesses just get a gentle "try again," never a red X.

4

Get rewarded

Stars, streaks, a sticker book, and badges — earned through play, never through waiting.

Built to feel good

Premium, playful, and honest

Every reward is earned through play. Nothing is designed to guilt, nag, or upsell a child.

7 game modes

Progressive Reveal, Clue Guess, Sound Guess, Silhouette, Quick Match, and more — same simple rules every time.

Adaptive difficulty

Gently nudges up or down based on how a session went — always visible, never hidden from a parent.

Sticker book

Every discovery becomes a collectible sticker — silhouettes show what's still out there to find.

No ads, no dark patterns

Nothing inside gameplay tries to sell anything or manufacture urgency. Rewards feel celebratory, not manipulative.

A real parent gate

Hold-to-unlock keeps settings, subscriptions, and progress out of reach of little fingers — no guessing required.

Offline-first

Every World that's installed works with zero internet — a real game, not a webpage pretending to be one.

For parents

Something you feel good handing over

No sign-up, no data collection by default, no ads to click through by accident. If you ever want cross-device backup, it's opt-in, anonymous, and lives behind a parent gate — never required.

  • No account for your childJust a local profile — an avatar, a nickname, an age.
  • Nothing collected by defaultNo analytics, no ad trackers, no third-party SDKs.
  • You're always in controlA parent gate protects settings, subscriptions, and progress.
